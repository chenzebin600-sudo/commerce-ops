# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Internal ecommerce operators, product researchers, ad analysts, and teammates on the same company network. They work under time pressure, compare marketplace data, inspect SKU price gaps, export reports, and collect ERP order data.

## Product Purpose
This tool centralizes cross-platform marketplace workflows: product-link competitor analysis, keyword TOP5 discovery, Mabangerp order collection, and Lazada Sponsored Max ad diagnostics. Success means users can move from raw links or exported Excel files to trustworthy tables, recommendations, and exports with minimal manual cleanup.

## Brand Personality
Precise, operational, composed. The interface should feel like a dependable internal command center: clear hierarchy, quiet confidence, dense but readable tables, and no decorative noise.

## Anti-references
Avoid marketing landing-page visuals, oversized hero sections, decorative gradients, glass cards, soft novelty illustrations, and one-note blue dashboard templates. Avoid cramped tables, weak contrast, confusing button styles, and hidden workflow states.

## Design Principles
1. Put workflow clarity before visual flourish.
2. Make data comparison scannable at speed.
3. Keep actions explicit and consistently placed.
4. Use restrained color only for state, selection, and priority.
5. Preserve trust by showing what was matched, filtered, or inferred.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Keyboard focus must be visible. Motion should be subtle and respect reduced-motion preferences. Tables must remain readable on smaller screens through horizontal scrolling rather than squeezed text.
